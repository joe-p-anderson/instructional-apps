Maxwell's Construction

Find the equipotentials of a dipole by adding the potentials of its two point charges.

Setup

Units: k·q = 36 V·(grid units) for each unit of charge, so a circle of potential V has radius r = k|q| / |V|. The charges are 8 units apart. Potentials are |V| = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 18 (radii 18, 12, 9, 7.2, 6, 4.5, 4, 3.6, 3, 2). For radii that fall between grid points, the nearest half unit is accepted and the circle is drawn at the exact radius. A larger charge uses only the potentials whose circles fit within r = 18.
