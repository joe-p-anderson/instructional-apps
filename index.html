Instructional Apps

Interactive simulations for lecture and lab, sorted by course. Each Tool works on its own and saves your work in this browser.

PHY 1200

Tools

  • Spacetime Diagrams Draw events, worldlines and rods in an Earth frame K and a rocket frame K′, with Lorentz-transformed coordinates, intervals and proper times.
  • Maxwell’s Construction Find the equipotentials of a dipole by adding the potentials of its two point charges.
  • Cross Product Practice Right-hand rule, basis vectors and the determinant method.

Activities

  • Spacetime Diagrams (Lab 10) Building the diagram and relativity of simultaneity, read by counting the rocket’s tilted grid lines.

PHY/EGR 3120

Tools

  • Oscilloscope Sinusoidal signals: amplitude, period and phase shift.
  • Bench Oscilloscope A virtual front panel for the lab’s TBS2072B and TDS2001C, with a function generator and filters feeding CH1 and CH2.
  • Fourier Series Build a periodic signal from its harmonics (Synthesis) or take it apart into its coefficients and spectrum (Analysis), through an optional RC low-pass or high-pass filter.
  • Phasors and Impedance Add two same-frequency sinusoids as phasors, head-to-tail on the complex plane and as curves in time, or drive a series RLC loop and see its impedances, phasors and KVL closure as ω sweeps.
  • Truth Table & Karnaugh Map Editor Build truth tables and simplify them with K-maps.

Activities

  • Sinusoidal Signals A guided oscilloscope activity that ends in a Report.
  • Frequency Domain: Fourier Series Build the square and triangle waves from their harmonics, compute the coefficients by hand, then filter the square wave with an RC low-pass and high-pass.
  • Phasors and Impedance Add two same-frequency sinusoids as phasors by hand, then work series RC and RLC circuits from impedance to current to device voltages, link RC to the Fourier low-pass, and find resonance.

PHY 4120 Experimental Physics

Tools

  • Vernier Caliper Practise reading a 0.05 mm vernier caliper: outside, inside and depth measurements.
  • Least Squares Fitting Linearize a physical model, fit a straight line by dragging its (m, b) handle, and judge it by SSE, R², residuals and error bars.
