Residuals  y − (m x + b)

Random scatter about 0: the straight line describes the data.
A curve or trend: wrong model or wrong axes. The line is systematically off.
One far-off point: an outlier. Check that measurement before trusting the fit.

This is unweighted least squares: error bars are drawn, but every point counts equally in the fit.

Parameter space

Each point here is a line. Drag to choose the slope m and intercept b. Rings are contours of equal error, labeled by R². The least-squares line sits at the bottom of the bowl. Fine zooms in near the bottom. The green patch is every line that passes through all the error bars: each one is consistent with the data.

R² is the fraction of the variation in y that the line accounts for. It does not tell you whether the model is right (a curved data set can still score near 1), and it is not an uncertainty on m. Look at the graph and the residuals first.
